# Public pricing guidance

Access pricing is discussed with the team. No numerical prices are approved for publication. The website has no self-service checkout.

The public page asks prospective organizers for a short assessment brief: format and frequency, approximate candidate count and simultaneous participation, dates, and requirements the team should confirm. These are conversation inputs, not a published pricing formula or qualification requirements.

Visitors can use the existing contact form (name, email and message required; organization and candidate range optional) or email team@amshq.in. Existing partners and sponsorship enquiries go to partners@amshq.in. The form is an enquiry, not an assessment reservation, meeting booking, or live-round support channel.

Before proceeding, a team should request written confirmation of scope, billing unit, included usage, total cost, applicable taxes, payment terms, changes and extra usage; device/setup responsibilities and support arrangements; and reviewer access, collection, retention and deletion terms.

## Owner decisions still required

The commercial owner must approve billing units, inclusions, usage limits, taxes, payment and cancellation terms, quote validity, and any support commitments before they are presented as offers. Data-handling commitments need approved policy details. Do not infer commercial entitlements from shipped product capabilities.

Earlier Pilot, Event, Institution and Enterprise descriptions were illustrative and are not approved plans. They must not be published as purchasable tiers or used to promise priority support, analytics, integrations, custom deployments, or other inclusions.
